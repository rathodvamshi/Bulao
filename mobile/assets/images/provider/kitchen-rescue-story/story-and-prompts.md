- Keep the upper 25% calm for the Bulao logo, location, notification and profile header.
- Put faces, dialogue and key action in the middle 55%.

- Preserve image proportions when integrating. Check the final crop on the native provider screen; generated safe zones are approximate.
- Short speech bubbles, consistent characters, warm cinematic 3D cartoon rendering.
